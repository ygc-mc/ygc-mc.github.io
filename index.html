About the Website

This website is a set of tools and reference pages for Minecraft players. The Damage Calculator works out exactly how much damage you'll take in PvP or PvE, factoring in your sword, armor, armor toughness, and enchantments like Sharpness and Protection. The Enchantments page is a quick reference for every enchantment in the game, organized by weapon and armor type. There's also a Shoutouts page recognizing some of my friends.
